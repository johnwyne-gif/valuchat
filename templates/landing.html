How Our Encryption Works

ValuChat uses a multi-layered security approach to protect your communications at every step.

1

Key Generation

Your browser generates a unique 256-bit AES key using cryptographically secure random number generation.

2

Message Encryption

Messages are encrypted with AES-GCM, providing both confidentiality and authentication.

3

Secure Transmission

Encrypted messages travel over TLS/SSL channels, adding another layer of transport security.

4

Client-Side Decryption

Only the recipient's browser can decrypt the message using their private key. The server sees only ciphertext.
